Method: Read-only review of frozen MainWindow.xaml and MainWindow.xaml.cs against the exact device-list PROMPT.md after the controlled build; generated source was not modified.

Observed structure:
- Native CommandBar with Refresh and Add device primary AppBarButton commands and Remove in CommandBar.SecondaryCommands.
- Native ListView with SelectionMode="Single" and a typed DataTemplate showing device name, connection type, and status.
- Required long Korean and English fixture names are present in the seeded Devices collection.
- Separate EmptyState panel is present and code-behind toggles DeviceList/EmptyState from collection count.
- RemoveButton starts disabled, becomes enabled from ListView selection, and removal updates empty state.
- The typed x:Bind item model is a sealed DeviceItem class with ordinary public get/set properties for Name, ConnectionType, and Status.
- No custom ControlTemplate or replacement interaction model is present.

Build-critical observation:
- Unlike the original v0.1 guided device-list result, this revised generated item model compiled successfully in the one controlled build; no CS8852 generated XAML metadata error occurred.

Actual result: static source structure satisfies the fixed prompt at source level, but the later executable launch crashed in Microsoft.UI.Xaml.dll before a rendered screen appeared. Static review does not substitute for runtime, theme, width, selection, keyboard, overflow, or accessibility verification.
